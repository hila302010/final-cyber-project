<p class="large-text">Welcome to OsinTech!</p> <br>
<p class="small-text">

At OsinTech, we harness the power of Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) to collect and analyze publicly available information based on a target domain name, company name, and country.</p> <br>
<p class="small-text">
Our custom-built Python scripts integrate with leading tools such as Shodan, WHOIS databases, and NetworksDB to extract critical technical and infrastructural data.
Beyond technical details, we delve into human intelligence by gathering insights from LinkedIn about the organization’s workforce. We also use GitHub and Google Dorks to uncover code repositories, exposed infrastructure components, and potential vulnerabilities linked to the company.
All gathered data is compiled into a detailed and structured report, providing a clear and comprehensive view of the organization's digital footprint, exposure, and security posture.
</p>

<p class="small-text">
Whether you're a cybersecurity professional, researcher, or analyst, OsinTech empowers you with actionable intelligence to better understand and assess online risks related to your organization.
